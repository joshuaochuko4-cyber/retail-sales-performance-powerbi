# retail-sales-performance-powerbi
Built a Retail Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI analyzing $8.9M revenue across 18,532 orders. Used Power Query for data cleaning and DAX for KPI measures including revenue, orders, and avg order value. Visualized country contribution and top products. Tools: Power BI · DAX · Power Query

## 📌 Project Overview
Designed and developed an interactive Retail Sales Performance 
Dashboard in Power BI to evaluate revenue trends, order behavior, 
and product performance across multiple countries.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Retail businesses handling large volumes of transactional data 
struggle to identify revenue patterns and top-performing products 
without a structured analytical view. This project transforms raw 
sales data into a clean, executive-level dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze retail sales data and deliver actionable insights around 
revenue performance, order trends, country contribution, and top 
products to support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization Best Practices

---

## 🔧 Data Preparation & Cleaning
- Cleaned raw dataset using Power Query
- Handled missing values and corrected data types
- Converted invoice date into proper date format
- Created time-based columns (Month & Year) for trend analysis

---

## 📐 Data Modeling & Measures
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders (distinct count of invoice numbers)
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value (Revenue ÷ Orders)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards** — Total Revenue ($8.9M), Total Orders (18,532),
  Total Quantity ($5.2M), Avg Order Value ($480.87)
- **Monthly Revenue Trend** — bar chart tracking performance 
  across all months
- **Orders Trend Over Time** — line chart for order volume tracking
- **Revenue by Country** — donut chart showing geographical 
  contribution
- **Top 5 Products** — ranked by total revenue
- **Product Detail Table** — breakdown by product, quantity, 
  revenue, and country
- **Interactive Slicers** — filter by Year and Country

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Total revenue reached **$8.9M** across 18,532 orders
- One primary country accounts for **92.99%** of total revenue
- Top performing product (Paper Craft) generated **$168K** alone
- A small group of products drives the majority of total sales
- Average order value sits at **$480.87** indicating mid-to-high 
  ticket purchases

---

## 💼 Business Impact
The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, 
identify top revenue drivers, and compare country-level contribution 
— all through interactive filtering for deeper, faster analysis.
